What could change the answer?

Ice Lance scaling, Fingers of Frost on bosses, mana regeneration, spell ranks, partial resistance and proc timing still need server evidence. A narrow sampling interval cannot resolve those uncertainties.

The archive uses a finite rotation search with separate training and evaluation seeds. Policies stay fixed during sensitivity tests. Safe-target leveling and AoE exclude incoming damage, deaths, pathing and group XP sharing; they do not establish practical XP/hour.
