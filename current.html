Playing Frost today


Keep Barrier's one-minute damage aura up, use Frostbolt from range, and spend useful empowered Ice Lances. Use five-stack Cone when safely in range; consider Clearcasting for expensive Cones in sustained combat. From levels 46–47 on the leveling path, use free, faster Missile Barrage channels when safe. Barrage-enhanced Missiles preserve Clearcasting.


The day-one Frost priority guide explains when each step unlocks, movement, shared cooldowns and emergencies. The leveling build stays 12/0/39 with Barrier at 40.


What the expanded Frost rotation research changes
A separate 17/3/31 level-60 probe favors modest Arcane Blast stacks: two for a short ranged fight, usually one for longer fights or safe Cone access. Long mana-limited fights favor selective five-stack Cones on Clearcasting. These candidates are not selectable in this simulator yet, and its displayed rankings do not include the expanded Frost search.
Ice Lance scaling can reverse whether a stationary model prefers using it. Keep empowered Lance in practical play; do not treat zero-scaling results or unverified last-charge “ghost” casts as proven techniques. Movement, incoming damage and the best Cold Snap schedule remain unmodeled or unoptimized.

What could change the answer?
Ice Lance scaling, Fingers of Frost on bosses, mana regeneration, spell ranks, partial resistance and proc timing still need server evidence. A narrow sampling interval cannot resolve those uncertainties.
